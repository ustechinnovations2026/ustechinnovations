Handheld NIR Spectroscopy

USTECH TrueLine 17EC

908–1676 nm Optical Range IP65 Protection >40,000h Lamp Lifespan ProChem Mobile BLE 1.4 kg Ergonomic Design

The TrueLine 17EC brings fast, non-destructive, and laboratory-grade Near-Infrared (NIR) analysis directly to the point of need. Eliminating the latency of sending physical samples to centralized laboratories, the TrueLine 17EC operates seamlessly alongside the ProChem Mobile application and USTECH cloud infrastructure. Whether receiving raw materials at the gate, optimizing processing lines, or sorting polymers in recycling yards, operators can execute high-precision quantitative measurements and qualitative identifications in seconds.

Applications & Value

Versatile Analysis Across Industries

Bringing fast and reliable NIR analysis directly to the point of need, eliminating conventional laboratory bottlenecks.

  • Feed & Animal Nutrition Rapid quality control from raw materials to finished feed. Enables instant on-site analysis of incoming grain shipments, feed ingredients, and finished pellets with multi-parameter calibration models (Moisture, Protein, Fat, Fiber, Starch, Ash).
  • Plastics & Polymers Recycling Fast material identification and quality assessment. NIR spectral information enables rapid classification of virgin and recycled polymers (PE, PP, PET, PS, ABS), flake sorting, and incoming raw material verification directly on the processing floor.
  • Textile & Fiber Identification Non-destructive fiber blend determination. Instant verification and sorting of natural, synthetic, and blended fibers without damaging expensive rolls or apparel samples. Eliminates destructive burn tests.
  • Chemical & Industrial Processing Flexible analysis and safe sampling for complex materials. Eliminates dangerous manual handling in hazardous environments while providing precise reaction endpoint determination and solvent identity verification.
Engineering Details

Field Hardware Architecture

Engineered specifically for reliable, routine field operations, the TrueLine 17EC combines specialized optics with a robust housing designed to withstand demanding industrial environments.

Precision Optical Engine

High-sensitivity linear InGaAs array optimized for the 908–1676 nm near-infrared window, featuring a sapphire window and >40,000h lamp.

Ergonomic Industrial Chassis

Center-balanced pistol grip with a lightweight 1.4kg aluminum/polymer design. Fully IP65 dust and water resistant.

ProChem Mobile Integration

Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE 5.0) pairing with Android/iOS for zero-latency operations, backed by constant USTECH Cloud Portal sync.

All-Day Field Power

High-density Li-ion battery supporting up to 8 hours of continuous operation on a single charge with 100-240V fast charging capabilities.
