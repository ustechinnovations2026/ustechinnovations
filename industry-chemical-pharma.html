Advanced Analytical Solutions for Pharmaceutical and Chemical Manufacturing

Pharmaceutical and chemical manufacturing require reliable analytical results, consistent product quality, and effective process monitoring. In regulated manufacturing environments, compliance with applicable regulatory requirements, Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP), and data integrity principles is also essential.

USTECH's NIR and FT-NIR analytical solutions support rapid material characterization, real-time process monitoring, and data-driven quality control throughout different stages of production.

By supporting Process Analytical Technology (PAT) applications such as raw material verification, blend uniformity monitoring, and reaction endpoint determination, these solutions can contribute to improved process understanding, product consistency, traceability, and operational efficiency.

The Challenges You Face

Pharmaceutical and chemical manufacturers work under strict quality and regulatory requirements, where reliable measurements, traceable data, and timely process information are essential. Yet many facilities still rely on offline methods such as chromatography (HPLC, GC) or wet chemistry, which introduce delays between sampling and decision-making.

01

Delayed Analysis & Limited Process Visibility

Conventional analytical methods require time for sampling, preparation, and testing. Changes that occur during production may not be detected in time, and access to process data remains limited.

02

Complex Process Conditions & Measurement Requirements

Temperature, pressure, chemical environment, material properties, and installation conditions all influence whether reliable measurements can be obtained during production.

03

Regulatory Compliance, Data Integrity & Traceability

GMP requirements call for reliable analytical data, controlled management of electronic records, complete documentation, and traceable production processes.

Analytical Solutions for Pharmaceutical and Chemical Manufacturing

USTECH offers a comprehensive range of NIR instruments and analytical software solutions designed to address the diverse analytical requirements of pharmaceutical and chemical manufacturing. From laboratory and on-site analysis to real-time process monitoring, USTECH's desktop, handheld, online, and inline NIR systems support material identification, composition analysis, and quality control.

By combining NIR instrumentation with advanced software for calibration model development, measurement management, analytical data visualization, and industrial automation integration, USTECH enables manufacturers to adapt analytical solutions to their specific materials, product formulations, and production requirements. 01. Raw Material Identification & Quality Assessment

Rapid Material Verification Before Production

Reliable raw material identification and quality assessment are essential for maintaining product consistency and meeting manufacturing requirements in the pharmaceutical and chemical industries.

USTECH's NIR and FT-NIR analytical solutions support rapid, non-destructive material identification, classification, and composition analysis of active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), excipients, and chemical raw materials.

With caliX chemometric software, manufacturers can develop their own application-specific spectral models based on their raw materials and analytical requirements. These models can help verify incoming materials, identify material variations, and support raw material acceptance decisions while complementing conventional laboratory analysis.

02. Blend Uniformity & Composition Monitoring

Monitor Material Distribution and Blend Consistency

Achieving consistent component distribution during blending is essential for maintaining product quality in pharmaceutical and chemical manufacturing. Variations in raw material properties and processing conditions can influence blend uniformity and final product characteristics.

USTECH's NIR and FT-NIR analytical solutions support the assessment of blend composition and uniformity. Using caliX, manufacturers can develop and validate calibration models tailored to their specific formulations, component concentrations, and analytical requirements.

Online and inline measurement configurations provide access to real-time analytical data at suitable measurement points, helping manufacturers identify compositional variations, evaluate blend consistency, and support the determination of appropriate blending endpoints.

03. Moisture Monitoring & Drying Process Analysis

Real-Time Insights into Moisture Changes

Moisture content is an important quality and process parameter in pharmaceutical granulation, drying, and chemical manufacturing. Variations in moisture levels can influence material stability, processing performance, and final product characteristics.

USTECH's online and inline NIR analytical solutions enable real-time monitoring of moisture changes at suitable stages of the production process. With caliX, manufacturers can develop moisture calibration models tailored to their specific materials, reference analysis methods, and operating conditions.

Continuous access to moisture data can support drying endpoint assessment, reduce dependence on periodic laboratory measurements, and help manufacturers maintain target product specifications. When integrated with compatible industrial automation systems, analytical results can also support automated process adjustments.

05. Custom Calibration Development & Analytical Data Management

Develop Your Own Calibration Models with caliX

Your Materials. Your Data. Your Calibration Models.

Pharmaceutical and chemical manufacturing applications have diverse analytical requirements. Differences in raw materials, product formulations, chemical compositions, and process conditions may require application-specific calibration models.

USTECH's caliX chemometric software gives manufacturers the flexibility to develop their own calibration models based on their materials, target analytical parameters, spectral data, and laboratory reference results, reducing dependence on predefined calibration models.

With caliX, users can manage spectral datasets, apply spectral preprocessing techniques, develop and validate calibration models, evaluate model performance, and update existing models as analytical requirements evolve.

Combined with ProChem and ProChem Viewer, these capabilities provide an integrated analytical workflow, from calibration model development and routine measurements to result visualization, historical data review, and industrial automation integration.
