Technology & Precision

No. Near-infrared spectroscopy is a non-destructive physical measurement: near-infrared light is directed at the sample and the reflected or transmitted light is analyzed. No chemical reagents are used, so there is no chemical waste and the sample is not consumed.

NIR is a secondary analytical method: it is calibrated against primary laboratory reference methods (such as wet chemistry). A well-built calibration can approach the accuracy of the reference method; how close it gets depends on the sample range and the quality of the reference data. Results take seconds instead of hours and need no reagents.

Filter-based systems measure only a few selected wavelengths, which limits their flexibility. Diode-array systems record the whole range at once with no moving parts, which makes them fast and robust in production environments. FT-NIR instruments use an interferometer and offer high spectral resolution and wavelength accuracy. Which design fits best depends on the application.

The ProLine17EC, ProLine17ECS and MasterLine17EC use an InGaAs photodiode array covering 950 nm to 1,650 nm (about 10,526 to 6,061 cm⁻¹). The ProLine2550 FT-NIR analyzer covers 1,350 nm to 2,550 nm (about 7,407 to 3,922 cm⁻¹). These ranges cover key absorption bands for C-H, O-H, and N-H molecular bonds, allowing for analysis of moisture, protein, fat, starch, fiber, and ash.

NIR is generally best suited to major and minor constituents rather than trace components. Whether a low concentration can be measured reliably depends on the sample matrix, the constituent and the precision of the reference method used to build the calibration, so we assess this for each application.

Devices & Hardware

The USTECH Tornado+ has an integrated water-cooling system that keeps the samples and grinding tools cool during grinding (water supply 2 L/min at 10 to 15 °C). This limits the heat generated during grinding, which can otherwise cause moisture loss, melt fats or drive off volatile components in high-moisture and high-fat samples.

Our NIR systems can measure solid and liquid samples. Solids such as grains, powders and pellets can be measured ground or unground; the MasterLine17EC scans the sample on a rotating sample table for a representative measurement. Liquids can be measured with liquid probes or with inline flow cells mounted directly on pipelines.

The USTECH StarchQC uses an amperometric method based on iodine absorption. A 1,000 mg flour sample is analyzed in an 8-minute cycle, and the damaged starch result is reported in AI %, NDS, UCDc, AACC and Farrand units and printed on the built-in printer. Results correlate strongly with enzyme-based reference methods.

Software & PLC Integration

Yes. ProChem sends results to the plant PLC over an Ethernet-based interface, and the ProLine2550 communicates over Ethernet (TCP/IP) and connects to factory PLC systems through its HTTP interface. The integration details for your control system are agreed during project planning.

ProChem calculates real-time chemical concentration values and sends them to the plant SCADA/PLC. The PLC compares these values against the setpoints (e.g., target moisture) and modulates control valves (e.g., steam or water spray) using PID loops.

ProChem provides user authentication, role-based access, an audit trail and electronic signatures, which support data-integrity requirements such as 21 CFR Part 11 and EU GMP Annex 11. Compliance itself is established by validating the complete system at your site; please contact us for the documentation available for your project.

Calibrations & Models

Yes. Through calibration transfer (standardization), a calibration model built in the caliX software suite can be transferred to other MasterLine17EC units by measuring a small set of common samples on each device.

USTECH offers comprehensive support. Through the caliX Suite, users can add new reference data and update their calibration models. We also offer remote chemometric support, where our specialists connect securely to review model performance, run validation tests and optimize calibrations.

An outlier is a sample whose spectral signature deviates significantly from the calibration dataset (due to foreign materials, extreme density changes, or parameters outside the model's range). Our software checks samples with metrics such as Mahalanobis distance, Hotelling T² and Q residuals, so that flagged readings can be reviewed before they are used for process decisions.

A robust custom PLS (Partial Least Squares) calibration needs reference samples that span the full range of values expected in routine use; the number required depends on the product and the parameter, and our team advises you on this for your application. For many applications, ready-made calibrations are also available as a starting point and are verified with your own samples.

Often, yes. Calibration databases from other instruments can be transferred with mathematical standardization methods, provided that a set of transfer samples is measured on both instruments. Feasibility depends on the wavelength ranges and data available, so we evaluate each case with you.

For quantitative models, the caliX Suite offers PLS (standard and robust), PCR, Ridge, Lasso, ElasticNet and robust Huber regression, with an AutoML search that sweeps model settings automatically. For data exploration and outlier screening it provides PCA together with Hotelling T², Q residuals and Mahalanobis distance.

Services, Maintenance & ROI

The payback period depends on your plant's throughput, raw material costs and how the measurements are used, so we do not quote a general figure. Savings typically come from reduced raw material giveaway, fewer out-of-specification batches, lower laboratory workload and optimized drying and mixing. Our team can estimate your own case together with you.

The ProLine17EC, ProLine17ECS and MasterLine17EC capture the whole spectrum at once with an InGaAs photodiode array and contain no interferometer or moving mirrors, so there is no periodic optical alignment for the user to perform. For the ProLine2550 FT-NIR analyzer, periodic performance checks are recommended.

Routine maintenance is minimal. For benchtop systems, operators should keep the sample window clean and regularly run a reference scan against the Spectralon reference standard; the MasterLine17EC lamp has a specified life of more than 40,000 hours. For inline sensors, lamp replacement and a check of the window wiper seals are usually planned during scheduled plant shutdowns.

Training needs are agreed with you for each project. Please contact our team to discuss training options for operators, laboratory staff and QA teams.

Installation time depends on the mounting point and your plant's infrastructure. We coordinate with your engineering team to mount the sensor during a planned maintenance window, followed by electrical connections, PLC communication checks and calibration verification.
