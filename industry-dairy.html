Precise Composition Analysis for Dairy Processing

Accurate and consistent monitoring of key quality parameters, including fat, protein, moisture, and total solids, is essential for product standardization and consistent quality in dairy production.

USTECH's NIR and FT-NIR analytical solutions provide rapid and reliable composition data at different stages of dairy production, from raw milk intake to in-process monitoring and finished product analysis. Inline NIR systems enable real-time monitoring of composition variations, while integration with compatible industrial automation systems allows analytical results to support product standardization.

These solutions help manufacturers maintain consistent product quality, optimize raw material usage, reduce production losses, and improve operational efficiency.

The Challenges You Face

Natural variability in raw milk composition, high hygiene requirements, and the need for consistent product standardization are among the main challenges in dairy production. Even small changes in fat, protein, and total solids content can affect product quality, production efficiency, and raw material usage.

In traditional quality control, samples are taken at set intervals and production decisions wait for laboratory results, which makes it difficult to follow changes as they occur during production. When analytical results are not available in time, adjustments to standardization are delayed and production losses can occur.

01

Milk Fat Standardization & Production Efficiency

Variations in milk fat content can make it challenging to maintain target product specifications. Without timely composition data, manufacturers may use more cream than necessary during standardization, increasing raw material costs and reducing production efficiency.

02

Delayed Analysis & Limited Process Visibility

Traditional laboratory analysis requires sample collection, preparation, and testing, which can delay access to critical composition data. In continuous dairy production, variations in product composition may occur between sampling intervals, making it difficult to identify deviations and take timely corrective action.

03

Regulatory Compliance & Process Traceability

Meeting food safety requirements and maintaining comprehensive production records are essential aspects of dairy processing. Periodic laboratory testing provides valuable quality data but offers limited visibility into variations occurring between measurements. Continuous process monitoring can complement laboratory analysis, improve traceability, and support more effective quality management and regulatory compliance.

Analytical Solutions Throughout Dairy Production

Effective quality control in dairy processing begins with raw milk inspection and continues through processing, standardization, and finished product analysis. USTECH offers NIR and FT-NIR analytical solutions tailored to the different stages and requirements of dairy production.

Desktop NIR analyzers support laboratory and at-line quality control, while online and inline systems enable real-time monitoring of product composition during production. Handheld NIR analyzers provide additional flexibility for rapid on-site measurements.

Together, these solutions help dairy manufacturers identify composition variations, support product standardization, maintain consistent quality, and improve production efficiency.

01. Raw Milk Reception & Quality Assessment

Know the Composition of Every Tanker.

Raw milk composition can vary depending on factors such as animal nutrition, seasonal conditions, and farm management practices. USTECH's NIR analytical solutions support rapid assessment of incoming milk composition, providing valuable information before raw materials enter the production process.

Timely analytical results help manufacturers evaluate incoming milk quality, identify compositional variations, and make more informed decisions regarding raw material acceptance and subsequent processing.

02. Cream Separation & Fat Standardization

Monitor Composition Changes in Real Time

Maintaining consistent fat content is central to dairy standardization. Inline NIR analyzers installed after the cream separator can monitor fat, protein, and total solids in real time at selected points of the line, giving operators continuous composition data instead of waiting for periodic samples.

Continuous access to analytical data helps manufacturers identify process variations, support standardization decisions, and avoid using more cream than necessary. When integrated with compatible automation systems through ProChem, measurement results can also support automated process adjustments.

03. Cheese, Yogurt & Whey Processing

Support Consistent Quality in Cheese, Yogurt and Whey

In cheese production, fat that remains in the whey stream is lost value. Inline NIR measurement on whey lines can help track residual fat and give operators earlier information on when process parameters may need to be reviewed.

For yogurt and fermented products, monitoring protein and total solids supports consistent product composition and helps reduce variations between production batches.

04. Spray Drying & Powder Quality

Keep Powder Moisture Close to Target

Milk powder moisture must stay within product specifications. Drying more than necessary reduces saleable weight and uses extra energy, while exceeding the moisture limit can affect product quality and shelf life.

Inline NIR analyzers installed at the spray dryer outlet or fluid bed exit provide continuous moisture data, helping operators keep moisture closer to the target. Handheld NIR analyzers can be used for rapid checks of finished powder.
